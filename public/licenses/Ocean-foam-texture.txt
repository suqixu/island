Ocean foam detail texture

File: src/assets/textures/water/ocean-foam-lace-v1.webp
Created: 2026-10-17, using OpenAI's built-in imagegen tool at the user's request.
Original generated image: 1254 x 1254 RGBA PNG.
Runtime encoding: lossless WebP; decoded RGBA bytes and dimensions are unchanged.

Purpose: a top-down, neutral white sea-foam lace texture with transparent gaps,
varied bubble sizes and dissolving edges. No water color or directional lighting
is baked into the material. Runtime code reads its alpha channel into one
512 x 512 R8 mask, generates mipmaps, and animates sampling coordinates inside
the existing ocean shader. Mirrored wrapping softens the generated tile edges.

The separate foam-crest-v1.png study under tmp/artifacts/ocean-foam-textures is a
preview asset and is not loaded by the production ocean material.
