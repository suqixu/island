Isola Toolbar

Derived from the bundled Ma Shan Zheng font in src/assets/fonts/poetry-calligraphy.ttf.
Contains only the original 诗 and 禅 glyph outlines plus .notdef, preserving the
original units per em, advance widths, ascender and descender. Rebuilt as a small
OpenType font with the family name Isola Toolbar for the startup toolbar.
